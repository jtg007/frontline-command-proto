# Playtest 1 — Quick Join (2 players, ~10 minutes)

**Goal:** One player is the **Commander** and places a waypoint. The other player is the **Driver**:
they find the waypoint **without voice callouts**, drive the tank there, and the Commander watches the ride.

## 1. Start

1. Unzip the client for your system and start `FrontlineCommand` (`.exe` on Windows, `.x86_64` on Linux).
2. The server address is already filled in. Type your name and **both pick team Blue**.
3. Click **Join**.

## 2. Play

| Role | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Commander** | Walk to the big blue **COMMAND VEHICLE** next to the spawn and press **C**. In the top-down view, **right-click the ground** to place a waypoint (the center hill is a good first target). WASD pans, Q/E rotates, mouse wheel zooms, C leaves the seat. |
| **Driver** | Walk to the **VEHICLE FACTORY** (blue roof) and press **E** at the tank. When the order arrives, follow the yellow beam or the arrow at the screen edge. W/S = throttle, A/D = steer, mouse = look around, E = get out. |

The game shows "Waypoint reached" to both of you when the tank arrives. Then swap roles and try again.

Other keys: **K** respawn · **F2** switch team · **Esc** menu · **F8** save a bug report.

## 3. Report (1 sentence is enough)

Post in Discord: build number (bottom-right corner), what you expected, what happened. For example:

> *Build b12, Playtest 1: the tank jittered when it drove up the hill (F8 zip attached).*

We are especially interested in:
- Was it **immediately clear** where the Commander wanted you to go?
- Did driving feel **sluggish** (delay between pressing W and moving)?
- Did the tank **jitter** or teleport for either of you?

**If something breaks, press F8.** A zip file is saved and its path is shown on screen (and copied to your clipboard).
Attach that zip to your message. It contains only the build, your system specs (OS/CPU/GPU), FPS statistics,
ping, and the last 200 game events. It contains no personal files.
